Website experiments and new product launches

When I joined the team, our goal was to increase the company's capitalization. The main revenue driver is, of course, our subscription services, but these require users to own our cameras. Consequently, we needed to boost sales on the e-commerce and increase conversion rates. To achieve this, we conducted numerous conversion experiments on different parts of the website. Here are a few of them.

Store product pages experiments

Before

  1. Low conversion rates from product pages despite high traffic.

  2. Subscriptions to the Petcube Care service were rare because users didn't pay much attention to it.

  3. Many users removed Petcube Care from their shopping carts, because it was unexpected.

After

  1. We added more compelling social proof to the page.

  2. Made the Petcube Care offer more attractive by introducing the Care Bundle (a free camera with a one-year subscription) and compared it to purchasing just the camera. This unified structure was also applied to other product pages.

  3. Changed the page layout to showcase all camera features in a video format.

Reviews

Later, we noticed that very few users were paying attention to the reviews presented on these pages. So, we decided to redesign the reviews by separating the text from the persuasive information.

Before

  1. The reviews appeared as a block of text that users had to read through, which they didn’t have time for.

  2. Users had to click through a slider to see more reviews.

After

  1. We separated the review text from the product rating, allowing users to quickly see the rating and the number of reviews with different ratings. They could still read the reviews if they wanted to.

  2. We displayed more reviews directly on the page.

Shopping Cart UX

Before

  1. The cart page was difficult to read, leading to a high drop-off rate.

After

  1. We redesigned the page by separating it into item sections and a summary, improving readability and scalability.

Cart Upsell Strategy

To boost sales, we decided to add upsells for each item in the cart. There were concerns that these upsells might annoy users, grab too much attention, and complicate their choices. So, we opted for a more personalized approach, adding no more than two upsells per item and limiting it to one upsell if there were multiple items in the cart. This made it easier for users to agree to the additional purchase.

We paid special attention to upselling bundles. We implemented a logic that allowed users to "upgrade" from one camera to two or three.

Result

  1. Bundles became the most popular item purchased from the cart, overall sales increased, and the drop-off rate at this stage of the purchase process decreased.

  2. We increased our sales 2x times

New Product Launches

During my time at Petcube, the company launched three new products. For each of these products, our team designed new landing pages and product pages. Our goal was to increase the scroll rate, ensuring that users would explore and understand the numerous features and benefits of our cameras.

To achieve this, I decided to incorporate more videos and animations, making it easier to explain each feature and making the pages more engaging. We developed a new scalable structure for the pages, which became the foundation for current and future landing pages.

Result

Homepage redesign

The number of products gradually increased as the company expanded, but over time, our main homepage lost its freshness and relevance, leading to significant sales challenges from this page.

Before

  1. Outdated media mentions and social proofs.

  2. A structure that didn't effectively accommodate a growing number of products.

  3. Storytelling that lacked focus and coherence.

After

  1. We introduced fresh and impactful social proofs.

  2. Restructured the homepage to focus on product categories rather than individual devices.

  3. Organized storytelling by highlighting the app and services, providing a clearer narrative.

These changes revitalized our homepage, making it more engaging and aligned with our expanded product range, ultimately boosting sales performance.
